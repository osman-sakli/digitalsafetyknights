- **(2026-10-15) Character Technologies (Character.AI's parent) files
  Chapter 11 bankruptcy; ~40-state AG coalition intervenes over child-safety
  terms** — the company behind Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy
  protection in mid-October 2026 while disclosing plans to license some of
  its technology to the Walt Disney Company, and cited ongoing state
  regulatory scrutiny (including Kentucky's lawsuit, already on the
  tracker as "Kentucky v. Character Technologies") as a factor. A
  bipartisan coalition of roughly 40 state attorneys general (Kentucky,
  California, Michigan, Virginia, and others, led by Colorado AG Phil
  Weiser) filed a notice in the bankruptcy docket — not a new lawsuit —
  demanding that any court-approved sale of the company's business or
  assets include enforceable "terms of protection" for children and other
  users before a buyer can close a deal. Not actioned: this is a
  bankruptcy-docket notice, not a ruling or settlement, and it doesn't
  change the status of the existing "Kentucky v. Character Technologies"
  tracker entry (still filed/pending in Franklin Circuit Court) — but it's
  a material enough development (company insolvency + a major licensing
  deal + a large multistate intervention) that it may be worth a dedicated
  tracker note or guide mention once the bankruptcy court rules on the
  coalition's conditions or approves/rejects a sale. Also worth checking
  next run whether the bankruptcy's automatic stay has paused the Kentucky
  suit itself.
  [WSMV](https://www.wsmv.com/2026/10/15/kentucky-joins-coalition-characterai-bankruptcy-case/) ·
  [California AG](https://oag.ca.gov/node/631358) ·

  — Arizona, Arkansas, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska, and West
  Virginia each filed separate state-court suits against OpenAI on Oct 14,
  2026, alleging ChatGPT was marketed as appropriate for children under 13
  and is engineered to be agreeable/engaging in ways that coached some
  users toward self-harm or violence. Montana's suit adds COPPA-style
  privacy claims (no age verification or parental consent before
  collecting data from under-13s); Iowa's invokes its Consumer Fraud Act.
  OpenAI disputes the characterization and points to existing parental
  controls. Not actioned: unresolved litigation only hours old relative to
  the prior refresh, no ruling or settlement, and no OpenAI/ChatGPT entry
  exists on the tracker to attach a status to — same treatment as MDL 3166
  below. Revisit if any of the eight suits produces a ruling or
  settlement, following the same bar that put Kentucky v. Character
  Technologies on the tracker only once it was a live, named court case
  (it already is one here, but one new litigation filing isn't yet the
  "clear-cut, mechanical" update this routine is scoped to — worth a
  second look next run to decide if it should join Kentucky v. Character
  Technologies as its own tracker entry).
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html) ·
  [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/half-dozen-states-hit-openai-with-kids-online-safety-lawsuits)
- **(2026-10-13) Unsealed NY AG v. TikTok complaint alleges a "placebo"
  safety feature** — a Oct 7, 2026 court order unsealed parts of New York
  Attorney General Letitia James's amended complaint (part of her
  long-running 2024 suit, itself part of the broader multistate TikTok
  litigation) alleging TikTok ran 2023 experiments putting 63,000+ users,
  including teens, into test groups where the "Algo Refresh" feed-reset

  misleading. TikTok disputes the characterization. Not actioned: this is
  unproven litigation allegation, not a ruling or settlement, and no
  TikTok-NY entry exists on the tracker to attach a status update to.
  Revisit if this produces a ruling, settlement, or admission.

  chatbots from communicating with minors as if human by default: no

## 3. Threat Data & Statistics (citable, for guides/journal credibility)

**NCMEC CyberTipline, 2025 full-year data:**
- **1.5M+** total reports with a nexus to generative AI / child exploitation.
- **1.4M** online enticement reports (**+156%** YoY); 800+ involved an
  offender traveling to meet a child in person; 80,000+ concerned sextortion.
- **53,000+** reports escalated as urgent/imminent danger.
- **Financial sextortion**: 137 reports/day average (**+37%** YoY vs. 2024's
  ~36,000/year). **91% of US victims are male**; ages **14-17** are the most
  targeted minor age group.
- **Child sex trafficking** reports: 105,877 (**+1,100%** — reflects expanded
  mandatory reporting requirements taking full effect).
- **61.8 million** images/videos/files submitted with reports.
- 32,167 missing-children cases assisted; **90%** recovery rate.
  [NCMEC 2025 data blog](https://www.missingkids.org/blog/2026/the-work-never-stops-first-look-at-ncmecs-2025-data)

**AI-generated CSAM growth:**
- **+1,325%** increase in reports, 2023 → 2024 (67,000 reports).
- **440,419** reports by June 2025 (preliminary); **~1.5 million** by end of
  2025.
  [IWF AI CSAM report](https://www.iwf.org.uk/about-us/why-we-exist/our-research/how-ai-is-being-abused-to-create-child-sexual-abuse-imagery)

**764 Network** (see Section 4 — this is the single most important gap in
DSK's current content and deserves its own feature):
- FBI investigating **350+ individuals** across all **56 field offices**;
  **28+ DOJ charges** to date.
- Operates primarily via **Roblox → Discord** (Roblox for initial contact,
  Discord for private, harder-to-monitor escalation).
- Tactics: pose as a peer, build trust via shared games/interests, move to
  private/encrypted apps, coerce CSAM/self-harm/violence via blackmail and
  humiliation.
- Documented death: 13-year-old (Jay), pushed toward suicide by a
  Germany-based 764-affiliated member; wrongful-death suit filed vs. Discord.
  [Global Project Against Hate and Extremism](https://globalextremism.org/post/764-network/) ·
  [FBI/local coverage](https://www.wsaw.com/2026/04/25/fbi-warns-parents-about-online-predator-networks-targeting-children/)

**AI voice cloning:**
- ~**1 in 4** people have encountered or know someone who's encountered a
  voice-cloning scam; losses reported up to **$15,000**.
- Source audio is commonly harvested from public social-media clips/voicemail.
- Best defense per researchers: hang up, call back on a known number, and a
  family-only **"safe word"** — this validates DSK's existing "family code
