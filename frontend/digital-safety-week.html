🛡️ Digital Safety Week Kit

A free, ready-to-run 5-day plan for your school — one Knight Rule a day, no prep beyond pressing play. Built for October, useful any month.

What's In The Kit

Five short, teacher-led moments — 5-10 minutes each, one per school day — each built around one real rule from our free Knight's Toolkit, paired with one of our free interactive tools so students practice it, not just hear it.

The 5-Day Plan

Monday · Knight Rule #1

Names, school, and address stay offline

Nice people online don't need your real name, school, or where you live to keep talking to you. Have students try building a "safe" profile in our simulator.

🗂️ Try Safe Profile Builder →
Tuesday · Knight Rule #2

Passwords are secrets — even from friends

Never share a password with anyone, no matter how well you know them. Let students test what makes a password actually strong.

🔐 Try Password Dojo →
Wednesday · Knight Rule #3

Urgency and secrecy are red flags

If a message rushes you or asks you to keep a secret from your parents, stop and tell a trusted adult. Paste example messages into our detector and spot the flags together.

🔍 Try Glitch Detector →
Thursday · Knight Rule #4

No in-person meetups without a trusted adult

Never meet someone from online in person without a parent knowing exactly where, when, and who with. Practice recognizing this ask in a safe, simulated chat.

💬 Try Chat Simulator →
Friday · Knight Rule #5

Telling a trusted adult is never the wrong move

If something feels off, or something already happened, tell someone — no one gets in trouble for telling. Close the week with exactly what to do next, in order.
