⚡ Quick Knight Tips
PARENTS

Set up device controls together with your child, not in secret — it builds trust and they're less likely to look for workarounds.

KIDS

If a message makes you feel rushed, secret, or a little bit weird — that's Shieldy's signal to stop and tell a trusted adult.

TEENS

Sextortion scams move fast on purpose. If it's happening to you: stop paying, stop responding, save the evidence, tell someone today.

EDUCATORS

Kids rarely report grooming directly — watch for sudden secrecy about a device, a new "friend" they won't name, or anxiety after being online.

PARENTS

"Made for Kids" AI chatbots still make mistakes. Review chat logs together weekly for anything under age 13.

KIDS

Nobody nice online ever needs your real name, your school, or your address to keep playing with you.

TEENS

An AI-generated ("deepfake") image of you is not your fault, and it is illegal in most countries — report it, don't hide it.

EDUCATORS

Roblox, Discord, and Snapchat all changed their safety defaults in the last year — re-check settings each semester, not just once.
