What This Is

Digital Safety Knights is a free nonprofit digital-safety education organization. Our mission is simple: every child, everywhere, deserves this education. We offer live school sessions — in-person across NJ and surrounding states, or virtual for any school in the world, in English, Spanish, or Turkish — plus a free 5-week classroom lesson plan, printable materials, and free interactive online tools. No purchase, subscription, or sign-up required for any of it.

What Students Actually Learn

  • Recognizing manipulation patterns in online conversations (urgency, secrecy, personal questions) — not just a list of "bad apps."
  • Practical privacy settings for the platforms they actually use (Roblox, Discord, Snapchat, iPhone/Android).
  • How to identify AI-generated content and deepfakes, and what current law (including the federal TAKE IT DOWN Act) means for them.
  • That telling a trusted adult is always the right move — never a punishment.

Why It's Free, and Why That's Safe

Nothing here is a lead-generation funnel for a paid product aimed at your students. The org's ethics guardrail is explicit: the safety education core stays free for every child, permanently — funding comes from grants, donations, and (separately) an optional paid family app for parents who want more, never from anything a student is required to use.

Privacy & Data

Our interactive tools use a nickname-only, no-account model built around COPPA — no student names, emails, or personal information are ever required or collected to use a quest, game, or tool. Live sessions collect nothing from students either.

Whole-Class Option

Prefer to run a quest as one projected, whole-class activity instead of individual devices? Open any quest with ?mode=classroom added to the link (e.g. digitalsafetyknights.org/quests.html?mode=classroom) — bigger text for the projector, no nickname or profile step, students vote by show of hands and the teacher clicks the class answer.
