Chapter One

It started with a question a lot of parents ask, and almost never get a real answer to.

"Is my kid actually safe on the games and apps they use every day?" Not a vague worry — a real, specific question. And every answer we could find was either a scary headline or a settings menu nobody had time to read.

Chapter Two

So instead of one more warning, we built a shield.

Not a lecture. Not a list of things to be afraid of. Something a kid would actually want to open — quests, badges, a character who says "Shield up" instead of "don't." Fear doesn't teach judgment. Practice does.

Chapter Three

Every good story needs someone who tries the trick on you first.

That's Glitch — not a real person, never based on one, just every scam and every red flag given one consistent, rememberable face. If you can spot Glitch's tricks in a game, you can spot them for real.

Chapter Four

Then came the guides, the Journal, the tools — because parents needed a shield too.

Family safety guides. A monthly deep-dive Journal. A Glitch Detector that never sends your messages anywhere. A safety audit. All free, on purpose — a real safety net shouldn't have a paywall between a family and the help they need.

Chapter Five — Today

We're still building. That's kind of the point.

New platforms show up every year, and so do new tricks. The Knights keep training for them — and every family that joins makes the whole thing a little bit stronger. This isn't a finished project. It's an ongoing one, and you're already part of it.
