Glitch Detector

Paste a message that seems suspicious. Shieldy will show you exactly which red flags it finds — instantly, and totally privately.

Nothing you type here is saved or sent anywhere — it's checked entirely on your own device.

What Shieldy looks for

  • Urgency — "hurry," "only seconds left," "act now"
  • Secrecy — "don't tell your parents," "just between us"
  • Personal questions — asking your name, age, school, or address
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers — free prizes, gift cards, "you won"
  • Money or password requests — asking to pay, verify, or send login info
