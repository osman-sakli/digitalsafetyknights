Digital Safety Knights

🌍 Chapters

A worldwide network of local Knights bringing digital safety education to schools and communities in their own country. Our mission: every child, everywhere, deserves this education — and a chapter starts with one person willing to bring it home.

Active Chapters

Real chapters, run by real people — updated live, never a made-up number.

What a Chapter Does

🏫

Local Sessions

Represents DSK at schools and community groups in your area — you lead, using our materials and training.

🗣️

Local Voice

Adapts our content to your country's platforms, laws, and language — you know your community best.

🤝

Local Network

Connects local schools, parents, and youth organizations into the wider DSK community and Knight Council.

🛡️

Backed by DSK

You're not starting from zero — guides, quests, training, and ongoing support come from the core team.
